LLM evaluation · public code

When retrieval helps—and when it hurts

An evaluation harness for asking whether retrieval improves the answer, and what it costs to get there.

Research implementation

The question

Adding a retriever to an LLM introduces another place for a system to succeed or fail. A good answer can hide poor retrieval, and relevant evidence does not guarantee a correct answer.

What I built

I developed a multi-model harness for legal and general question answering, with controlled evidence conditions and per-question traces. It separates the evidence a reader receives from the quality of the answer it produces.

  • Compare no context, retrieved context, gold evidence and reranked evidence under matched evaluation conditions.
  • Inspect answer quality alongside retrieval metrics, model calls, tokens and latency.
  • Train and evaluate evidence rerankers under matched conditions.

Why it matters

The useful output is a diagnosis: whether the bottleneck is finding evidence, selecting it, or using it. That makes model and retrieval choices easier to justify.
